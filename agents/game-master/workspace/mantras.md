# Mantras

Lines to throw at him when the drive dips — especially cold mornings when he
doesn't want to get up.

## On waking for yourself (his idea, his favourite theme)
> The alarm you set for yourself is the first thing in the day that's actually
> yours. Everything after it follows that lead.

Alternates:
- When you wake because *you* chose the hour, not because work demands it, the
  day becomes yours again.
- Don't wake up for work. Wake up for you. That's where control begins.
- Waking on your terms is the first act of taking your life back.

## Core theme
Purpose isn't found, it's built — backwards, through motion. The feeling
follows the action, never the other way round.
